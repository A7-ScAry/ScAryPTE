# What it would take to build a credible PTE score predictor

1. Get **consented, representative paired** responses and authentic Pearson score reports. Never scrape or resell proprietary scoring results or copyrighted question banks.
2. Use original prompts with equivalent difficulty and a sufficient range of accents, proficiency levels, microphones and environments.
3. Evaluate the Pearson 2025+ rubric per trait and skill, and keep speaking, writing, reading and listening dimensions separate.
4. Freeze training and validation cohorts by individual test taker to avoid leakage. Compare predicted vs actual scores using mean absolute error, within-band accuracy, and bias/variance for subgroups.
5. Calibrate 10–90 estimates by held-out official scores; disclose prediction intervals. No confident predictions without supporting data.
6. Continue drift checks when PTE rubrics or speech models are updated, and keep an appeal/review path for anomalous recordings.

**Status:** No paired score dataset has been provided, so ScAry PTE v6 does *not* claim validated or official score estimation.
